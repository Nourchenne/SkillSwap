Frequently Asked Questions

How do I earn more credits?

Offer your skills and teach others! Every hour you teach earns you 1 credit. You can also earn bonus credits through referrals and community participation.

What if I don't have any teachable skills?

Everyone has something to teach! Whether it's your native language, a hobby, a professional skill, or even help with everyday tasks - your knowledge is valuable to someone.

Is SkillSwap really free?

Yes! SkillSwap operates on a time-based credit system, not money. Everyone starts with 5 free credits, and you earn more by teaching. No subscription fees, no hidden costs.

How long does an exchange take?

It varies! Skills are offered in 1-4 hour sessions. You coordinate directly with your exchange partner to find a time that works for both of you.

What if an exchange goes wrong?

We have a dispute resolution system. If both parties don't confirm completion, credits aren't transferred and the exchange can be reviewed by our community moderators.
