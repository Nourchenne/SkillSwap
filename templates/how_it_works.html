How SkillSwap works

Trade skills using time credits. Learn something new while sharing what you know.

Browse skills Offer a skill

The flow in 6 steps

1. Create your account

Sign up for free. You start with 5 time credits (1 credit = 1 hour).

💡 Time credits, not money

2. Offer your skills

Create offers for things you’re good at: coding, languages, music, cooking…

  • Set availability and teaching style
  • Meet in-person or over video
  • Teach 1:1 or small groups

3. Find skills to learn

Browse the marketplace and pick what you’ve always wanted to learn.

💻 Programming 🎸 Music 🗣️ Languages 🎨 Design 🍳 Cooking 📸 Photography

4. Propose an exchange

Use your credits to request a session. The teacher reviews and accepts.

Example: 2-hour photography session 2 credits

Credits are held until both confirm completion.

5. Learn and teach

Coordinate, complete the session, and confirm completion together.

Learner: spend credits Teacher: earn credits

6. Leave a review

Reviews build trust and help great teachers stand out.

⭐ Reputation matters

FAQ

How do I earn more credits?

Teach others. Every hour earns 1 credit. You can also earn bonus credits via referrals and community participation.

Is SkillSwap free?

Yes. SkillSwap runs on time credits, not money. Everyone starts with 5 free credits.

What if an exchange goes wrong?

If both parties don’t confirm completion, credits aren’t transferred and moderators can review the case.

How long is a session?

Offers range from 1–4 hours. Coordinate directly with your partner to find the best time.
